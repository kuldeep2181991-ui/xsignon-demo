// OUR PRODUCTS

Purpose-Built Cybersecurity Products for Identity, Compliance, and Quantum Readiness

XSignOn delivers cybersecurity solutions built on proven standards, designed to integrate with your existing technology stack.

  • Data Security Posture Management (DSPM)

    Unified Identity Security Posture Management Across Every Environment

    Posture One is XSignOn's data security posture management (DSPM) platform — giving security teams continuous visibility into identity risk, access entitlement drift, and data exposure across AWS, Azure, GCP, and on-premises systems. Built on Zero Trust principles, Posture One continuously monitors access entitlements, detects privilege creep, flags policy violations, and triggers automated remediation workflows. Unlike point-in-time access reviews, Posture One provides a real-time security posture score — enabling CISOs and IT Directors to demonstrate measurable, continuous improvement to boards, auditors, and regulators. Posture One also extends coverage to AI agent identities, providing a complete picture of who — and what — has access to sensitive data and systems.

    Zero Trust DSPM AI Identity Coverage Multi-Cloud Real-Time Posture Scoring
  • Passwordless Authentication · FIDO2

    Enterprise Passwordless Authentication Built on FIDO2 Standards

    XSignOn's Passwordless Authenticator replaces legacy password-based authentication with FIDO2-compliant, biometric and device-bound authentication — eliminating the most common vector for credential-based attacks without degrading the user experience. Credentials are cryptographically bound to the user's device and never transmitted over a network — making phishing, credential stuffing, and man-in-the-middle attacks structurally impossible for protected resources. Integrates with Active Directory, Azure AD, Okta, and all major identity providers. SOC 2 and FIDO2 compliant. Designed for 14+ Industries implementations across mid-market enterprises.

    FIDO2 Compliant Biometric Auth Phishing Resistant Zero Password SOC 2 Ready
  • Passwordless Authentication · FIDO2

    Continuous Compliance Automation for SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR

    Compliance Automation is XSignOn's platform for continuous compliance monitoring and automated audit preparation across SOC 2 Type I & II, HIPAA Security Rule, GDPR, CCPA, NIST CSF, and FedRAMP. Rather than treating compliance as a calendar-driven event, Compliance Automation maintains a continuous evidence collection pipeline — automatically documenting control effectiveness, flagging policy exceptions, and generating audit-ready reports on demand. This approach reduces the cost and disruption of annual audits while producing a stronger, more defensible compliance posture year-round.

    SOC 2 Type I & II HIPAA GDPR CCPA FedRAMP Continuous Evidence Collection
