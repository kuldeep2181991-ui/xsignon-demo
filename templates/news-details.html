Elevate Enterprise Security with Identity-First Controls

Healthcare and finance organizations carry some of the most sensitive data in the world — patient health records, payment data, financial transactions, and identity details. At the same time, they face some of the highest breach costs: The average healthcare breach costs $10.93M per incident (IBM 2023). Financial services face the fastest escalation, with attackers moving laterally to compromise multiple systems. In both industries, security starts with identity. Who has access, when they have it, and how it’s monitored can mean the difference between trust and breach. That’s why modern enterprises must adopt Identity-First Security, unifying Identity Access Management (IAM) and Privileged Access Management (PAM) into one powerful platform.

Ensurity: Hardware-Based Identity Security

As a trusted XSignOn partner, Ensurity Technologies provides hardware-based passwordless authentication and biometric identity protection that forms the foundation of secure access. Ensurity’s patented FIDO2-certified hardware keys, such as ThinC-A Key, combine biometric fingerprint verification with secure enclaves to eliminate credential theft and phishing risks entirely.

  • Passwordless Biometric Authentication: Hardware-based login with FIDO2-compliant anti-spoofing sensors.
  • Zero Trust Alignment: Ensures every login is verified locally before access is granted.
  • Tamper-Resistant Hardware Security: Protects credentials against cloning or extraction.
  • Cross-Platform Support: Works seamlessly across Windows, Azure AD, Linux, VPNs, and hybrid environments.

With Ensurity integrated by XSignOn, enterprises can confidently implement passwordless, hardware-rooted authentication for doctors, advisors, executives, and IT admins, reducing friction while eliminating the password attack surface.

WhiteSwan: Software-Defined Access Control

WhiteSwan Security, another key XSignOn partner, extends the identity-first model into a software-defined Zero Trust framework, unifying IAM, PAM, and policy enforcement across hybrid cloud and on-prem environments.

  • Adaptive Access Control: Risk-based authentication using contextual factors such as user behavior, device trust, and location.
  • Privileged Access Orchestration: Centralized PAM policies and behavioral monitoring for IT admins and executives.
  • Dynamic Policy Enforcement: AI-driven micro-policies that adapt in real time to evolving threats.
  • Audit & Compliance Automation: Continuous reporting for HIPAA, SOX, PCI DSS, and GDPR.
  • Seamless Integration: Works with existing IAM, directory, and SIEM tools.

With WhiteSwan’s platform integrated through XSignOn, organizations achieve centralized visibility and real-time policy enforcement, ensuring least-privilege access, continuous verification, and rapid compliance alignment across every identity.

Why Identity-First Security Matters in Healthcare and Finance

Identity has become the new perimeter. Every patient, customer, doctor, employee, or third-party user is a potential access point. Attackers know this, which is why they target identities first.

  • An Identity-First Security Model ensures:
  • Passwordless, frictionless login for staff, patients, and customers
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for stronger protection.
  • Least privilege access to sensitive systems and applications
  • Continuous monitoring of privileged accounts and admin sessions
  • Compliance readiness for HIPAA, PCI DSS, SOX, and GDPR

For healthcare, this means doctors can access patient records securely without delay, while administrators are protected against phishing and credential theft. For finance, it means customer transactions, payment systems, and trading platforms are safeguarded against unauthorized access.

Identity Access Management (IAM)

IAM provides the foundation for secure, scalable access across healthcare and finance enterprises. Key features include: Passwordless Biometric Authentication – Enable secure login with FIDO2 biometric keys and anti-spoofing fingerprint sensors.

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) – Certificate-based smart cards, TACACS, and contextual login checks across platforms.

Cross-Platform Compatibility – Seamless login across Azure AD, Windows, Linux, macOS, RDP, VPNs, and hybrid Active Directory.

Centralized Key Management – Activate or deactivate users, log access events, and integrate with SIEM for real-time monitoring.

Flexible Deployment – SaaS, on-premises, or air-gapped environments for compliance-driven sectors like healthcare and finance.

IAM reduces friction for frontline workers, advisors, and customers — while ensuring attackers can’t exploit weak or reused passwords.

Privileged Access Management (PAM)

PAM is critical for protecting high-value accounts — IT admins, system operators, financial controllers, and healthcare executives.

Key features include:

Administrative Privileged Access – Role-based policies and smart card authentication for IT staff.

Business Privileged Access – Conditional workflows and behavioral analysis for executives and finance leaders.

Session Recording & Monitoring – Track keystrokes, capture screens, and audit every privileged action for accountability.

Password Vaulting & Rotation – Automatically rotate and encrypt service account passwords to eliminate risk.

Flexible Deployment – Cloud, on-premises, or hybrid to match regulatory requirements.

For hospitals, PAM prevents unauthorized access to electronic health records (EHRs) and connected medical devices. For banks, it protects payment systems, customer account data, and financial transaction platforms from insider or external misuse.

Unifying IAM and PAM with Zero Trust

Instead of siloed solutions, healthcare and finance organizations need unified identity-first controls, bringing IAM and PAM together under Zero Trust principles. This means:

  • Every login is verified — human or machine.
  • Every privileged session is monitored and recorded.
  • Every access request is least privileged by design.
  • Every compliance framework is supported with automated reporting and monitoring.

By unifying IAM + PAM, organizations gain a single pane of glass for identity, access, and compliance, reducing complexity and strengthening trust.
