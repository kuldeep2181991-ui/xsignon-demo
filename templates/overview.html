  • What is XSignOn and how does it work?
    XSignOn is a unified cybersecurity orchestration platform that helps businesses manage identity, access, and compliance from a single dashboard. It integrates with your existing tools to provide real-time visibility, automate security processes, and reduce operational complexity.
  • How does XSignOn improve compliance management?
    XSignOn enables continuous compliance by automating controls for standards like SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, and ISO 27001. Instead of point-in-time audits, you get real-time monitoring, alerts, and audit-ready reports at any time.
  • Can XSignOn replace multiple security tools?
    Yes. XSignOn is designed to eliminate tool sprawl by consolidating multiple security functions into one platform. This reduces costs, simplifies management, and improves overall security posture.
  • How does XSignOn handle AI-related security risks?
    XSignOn helps identify and manage Shadow AI risks by monitoring unauthorized AI usage, enforcing governance policies, and protecting sensitive data across your organization.
  • Is XSignOn suitable for small and mid-sized businesses?
    Absolutely. XSignOn is built to democratize enterprise-grade security, making advanced cybersecurity accessible, scalable, and cost-effective for SMBs and growing companies.
