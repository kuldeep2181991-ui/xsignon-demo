Privacy & Data Protection at XSignOn

At XSignOn, privacy is not just a policy — it is a core part of our cybersecurity foundation. We are committed to protecting your personal and business information through secure technologies, transparent practices, and strict governance standards.

As a unified cybersecurity platform, we ensure that every piece of data collected is handled responsibly, used only for legitimate purposes, and protected against unauthorized access, misuse, or exposure.

What Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to deliver our services effectively. This may include basic contact details such as your name, email address, company information, and technical data like IP address, browser type, and device information for security and analytics purposes.

  • Deliver and improve cybersecurity services
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests
  • Detect and prevent security threats
  • Ensure compliance with legal obligations

Data Security & Protection

We implement advanced security measures aligned with Zero Trust architecture to safeguard your data across all touchpoints.

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Role-based access controls
  • Continuous monitoring and threat detection
  • Secure infrastructure and compliance alignment

Data Sharing & Transparency

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Information is only shared with trusted partners when necessary for service delivery, legal compliance, or security purposes.

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of communications
  • Control cookie and tracking preferences

By using our platform, you trust us with your data — and we take that responsibility seriously. Our commitment is to maintain the highest standards of privacy, security, and transparency at all times.
