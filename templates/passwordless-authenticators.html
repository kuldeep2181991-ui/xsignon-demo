Questions Teams Ask Before Deploying

  • Is this just one hardware authenticator?
    Not at all. One passwordless authenticator strategy can combine XSense, AMS, and device choices, giving enterprises identity control, lifecycle oversight, and deployment flexibility at scale.
  • Can shared workstations stay cost-efficient?
    Yes. AUTH Desktop supports up to three users on one key, preserving biometric authentication, stronger accountability, faster turnover, and lower hardware spend across shifts daily.
  • Does it fit regulated financial environments?
    Yes. AUTH BioPro extends FIDO2 security keys with PIV and PKI support, helping regulated teams strengthen identity theft prevention and tighter machine restrictions for access.
  • How do we manage devices at scale?
    AMS handles provisioning, monitoring, reassignment, retirement, and audits, while XSense coordinates policies across cloud, on-premises, and air-gapped environments from one control layer for administrators.
  • How does this reduce credential risk?
    Because cryptographic login stays on the device, FIDO2 passkeys support credential theft prevention and make phishing, replay, and reuse attacks harder daily for employees everywhere.
