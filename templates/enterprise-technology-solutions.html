Common Questions About Enterprise Technology Solutions

  • What Happens If An Employee Loses A Device?
    XSignOn supports a secure identity recovery workflow, so a lost hardware authenticator does not force weak passwords, permanent lockouts, or risky helpdesk improvisation during recovery.
  • Can Users Still Authenticate Offline?
    Because FIDO2 authentication and WebAuthn authentication rely on local cryptographic challenge handling, users can unlock approved devices even when network connectivity temporarily disappears for work.
  • Will This Work With Our Current IdP?
    Yes. XSignOn is built for identity provider integration, overlaying Okta, Ping, or Entra ID, so passwordless authentication strengthens access without replacing core directories or policies.
  • How Does Onboarding Work For New Hires?
    IT teams can pre-register a hardware authenticator before day one, letting new employees activate approved devices quickly and enter authorized apps with less setup effort.
  • Does XSignOn Store Biometric Or Secret Data?
    Biometric data stays on the user device, while public-key design supports zero trust authentication, stronger privacy, and passwordless authentication without storing reusable secrets on servers.
