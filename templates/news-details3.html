Non-Human Identities, and the Future of Cyber Risk: Embracing a Dynamic Security Model

Weekends are always a time to reset, but sometimes even a casual conversation can spark big ideas. Over coffee in DFW, the topic shifted to something shaping the future of cybersecurity, the need for a Dynamic Security Model.

Why Dynamic Security Models Are Critical

Agentic AI builds on the rise of Generative AI (Gen AI), which powers chatbots and other natural language tools. But while Gen AI relies on human prompts, Agentic AI uses autonomous agents that operate on their own.

These agents:

  • Understand tasks
  • Choose the right tools
  • Execute actions without step-by-step instructions

In cybersecurity, this means AI agents could:

  • Detect suspicious behavior in real time
  • Block risky access attempts automatically
  • Trigger alerts and isolation policies before a breach spreads

This approach fits naturally into Zero Trust Security models, where no user or system is trusted by default.

Agentic AI has introduced non-human identities (NHIs): APIs, bots, autonomous agents that:

  • Self-initiate transactions
  • Scale resources in real time
  • Continuously evolve access needs
  • Carry context and memory into each interaction

A Dynamic Security Model (DSM):

  • Continuously identifies and governs both human and non-human identities
  • Automates provisioning and remediation across every access layer
  • Acts as the digital nervous system for adaptive, machine-driven environments

This aligns with XSignOn’s vision: moving beyond static defenses to intelligent, identity-first protection that keeps pace with autonomous systems.

Data Security Posture Management (DSPM): The Missing Layer

Traditional IAM and perimeter security were designed for human users. But in an Agentic AI world, the risk profile changes:

🌐 Identity Explosion – Thousands of NHIs with elevated access 🕵️ Shadow Permissions – Silent privilege creep across automated processes 🎭 AI-Augmented Threats – Fake “services” posing as legitimate actors ⚡ AI Debt – Complex architectures breaking under machine-driven demand.

DSPM provides the visibility, governance, and assurance needed for this new reality. It monitors how data moves, who (or what) can access it, and ensures governance across the entire data lifecycle, making it a cornerstone of resilient security in the AI era.

Why Risk Quantification & Insurance Models Break Without It

Boards and insurers want clear numbers on risk. But current models assume:

  • Static assets
  • Human-led transactions
  • Predictable failure points
  • A stable, accurate CMDB

Agentic AI breaks those assumptions. Risks now evolve by the hour as autonomous services scale and interact. Attack surfaces multiply without human oversight. Without DSPM-driven telemetry, cyber insurance is mispriced and risk is misjudged. Enterprises must adopt dynamic access and role provisioning to stay aligned with both regulators and insurers.

The CXO Playbook for the Agentic AI Era

  1. Adopt a Dynamic Security Model (DSM): Adapt security controls for both human and non-human identities.
  2. Implement DSPM at Scale: Continuously monitor and remediate data risks tied to autonomous agents.
  3. Extend Risk Quantification: Factor in AI-driven decisions and service-to-service traffic.
  4. Engage with Insurers: Provide dynamic, transparent risk data to secure better coverage.

Enterprises built for the SaaS era aren’t ready for the AI era, one that is fluid, probabilistic, and constantly shifting. Security models must evolve in step.
